# Onecore Pharma — Image Art Direction & AI Prompts (Indian Context)
**Capturing the Lilly Aesthetic with Authentic Indian Subjects & Environments**

---

## 1. Visual Philosophy & Photography Rules

To match the emotional resonance and prestigious aesthetic of **Eli Lilly (lilly.com)** in an authentic **Indian healthcare context**:

* **Authenticity & Dignity**: Subjects must look like real, dignified Indian citizens, patients, doctors, and scientists. Avoid artificial plastic smiles, dramatic poses, or generic stock-photo stereotypes.
* **Lighting & Tone**: Warm, natural golden-hour daylight, soft window light, subtle cinematic lens falloff (shallow depth of field, f/1.8–f/2.8), rich and authentic Indian skin tones.
* **Color Grading**: Film-like color palette, warm stone and neutral architectural tones, no oversaturated greens or blues.
* **Environments**: Modern urban Indian clinical environments, contemporary Indian homes with warm daylight, and state-of-the-art pharmaceutical cleanroom laboratories.

## 6. How to Provide Generated Images

Once you generate images from ChatGPT / Midjourney:
1. Save the image file matching the target filename in `public/assets/` (e.g. `hero-healthcare.jpg`, `cytos.jpg`, etc.).
2. You can drop them into the `public/assets/` directory directly or share them in batches.
3. Every page has already been programmed with dynamic fallback handling and relative URL resolution via `assetUrl`, so as soon as you save the images, they will instantly display on the website!
