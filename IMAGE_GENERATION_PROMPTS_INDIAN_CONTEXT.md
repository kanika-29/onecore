# Onecore Pharma — Image Art Direction & AI Generation Guide
**Capturing the Editorial Spirit & Emotional Resonance of Lilly in an Authentic Indian Context**

---

## 1. The Core Visual "Sense" (What We Are Borrowing from Lilly)

When studying [Eli Lilly's imagery](https://www.lilly.com/), what makes it powerful is **not** any specific pose, shirt color, or actor. It is the **underlying emotional and artistic sense**:

1. **Dignity & Quiet Strength**: Patients and caregivers are portrayed with hope, resilience, and humanity — never as helpless victims or exaggerated stock-photo caricatures.
2. **Authentic, Unposed Candid Moments**: Looks like genuine documentary photography from real life. Natural smiles, thoughtful expressions, subtle gestures, and spontaneous connection.
3. **Cinematic Natural Lighting**: Warm morning or late-afternoon sunlight, soft window backlight, gentle contrast, and natural skin tones. No harsh artificial flash or plasticky AI gloss.
4. **Cinematic Depth of Field**: Softly blurred backgrounds (`f/1.8` to `f/2.8`) that keep the focus intimate and human while hinting at a modern, clean environment.
5. **Modern Indian Healthcare Reality**: Clean, contemporary Indian urban clinical spaces, welcoming family homes, and state-of-the-art pharmaceutical research laboratories.

## 3. How to Apply Your Generated Images

Whenever you generate an image that you love:
1. Save it to `public/assets/` using the specified filename (e.g. `hero-healthcare.jpg`, `cytos.jpg`, etc.).
2. The website uses dynamic relative asset resolution (`assetUrl`), so your new images will immediately replace the placeholders and display across all cards and banners.
